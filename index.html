Projects

ME Freshman Design Project: Spring, 2026

Figs.1,2 2nd place photo and CAD model of vehicle

For the Spring, 2026 semester, I had the opportunity to lead a team of five engineering students in a design competition where we were tasked with designing, testing and building an RC vehicle which could pick up and dispense balls of a variety of different sizes and masses. We worked as a team, collaborating on design choices and we worked together to solve various technical issues throughout the project. In the end, we earned a 2nd place spot out of the 23 design teams in the competition.

My focus within the project

I was the team lead in the project, so a large part of my job was to keep tabs on the project's status and make sure we were on track to meet deadlines and deliverable dates. This meant building detailed Work Breakdown Structures and Gantt charts to keep tabs on our project's tasks and progression.

Mechanically, my job was to do all of the programing, wiring and final assembly of the vehicle. This entailed a lot of self teaching, as I learned about DC circuitry and worked through motor torque issues, brownout issues and programming related issues.

I also helped with the CAD modeling, but most of my involvement here was small dimensional tuning as I tested components and assembled the final vehicle.
