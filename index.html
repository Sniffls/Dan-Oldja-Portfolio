ME Freshman Design Project: Spring, 2026

Figs.1,2 The design team and the CAD model of the vehicle

For the Spring, 2026 semester, I had the opportunity to lead a team of engineering students in a design competition where we were tasked with designing, testing and building an RC vehicle which could pick up and dispense balls of a variety of different sizes and masses. We ended up getting 2nd place out of the 23 design teams in the competition.

My focus within the project

I was the team lead in the project, so a large part of my job was to keep tabs on the project's status and make sure we were on track to meet deadlines and deliverable dates. This meant building a detailed Work Breakdown Structure and Gantt chart to keep tabs on our project's progression.

Mechanically, my job was to do all of the programing, wiring and final assembly of the vehicle. This meant I did a lot of self teaching as I learned about DC circuitry and worked through motor torque issues, brownout issues and programming related issues.

I also helped with the CAD modeling, but most of my involvement here was small dimensional tuning as I tested components and assembled the final vehicle.
